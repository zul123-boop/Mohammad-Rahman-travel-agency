About Maldives

The Maldives is an archipelago of 1,190 coral islands grouped into 26 atolls in the Indian Ocean. Known for its impossibly blue lagoons, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant coral reefs, it is the ultimate tropical paradise — perfect for honeymooners, divers, and anyone seeking serene luxury.

Each resort island is a private world of its own, offering overwater villas, world-class diving, and an unmatched sense of isolation and peace.

Best Time to Visit

November to April — The dry northeast monsoon offers sunny weather, calm seas, and perfect diving conditions. May to October is the wet southwest monsoon with occasional rain showers but fewer crowds and lower prices.

Key Locations

  • Malé — Capital city & cultural hub
  • North Male Atoll — Premium resort islands
  • Baa Atoll — UNESCO Biosphere Reserve
  • Ari Atoll — Whale shark hotspot
  • Laamu Atoll — Surfing & pristine reefs
