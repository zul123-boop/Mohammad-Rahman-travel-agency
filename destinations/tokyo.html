About Tokyo

Tokyo is a mesmerizing metropolis where centuries-old temples stand beside neon-lit skyscrapers. Japan's capital offers an unparalleled blend of traditional culture — tea ceremonies, sumo wrestling, and Shinto shrines — alongside cutting-edge technology, anime culture, and world-renowned cuisine.

From the bustling Shibuya crossing to the serene Meiji Shrine, Tokyo rewards every curiosity and exceeds every expectation.

Best Time to Visit

March to May & October to November — Cherry blossom season (late March-April) and autumn foliage (November) are magical. June-July brings rainy season, while August-September can be hot and humid.

Key Locations

  • Shibuya — Iconic crossing & nightlife
  • Shinjuku — Neon district & gardens
  • Asakusa — Senso-ji Temple & Nakamise Street
  • Akihabara — Anime & electronics heaven
  • Harajuku — Fashion & youth culture
