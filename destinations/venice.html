About Venice

Venice is unlike any other city on earth — built on 118 small islands connected by over 400 bridges and navigated by canals instead of streets. This UNESCO World Heritage site is a living masterpiece of architecture, art, and history, where every bridge tells a story and every corner reveals a hidden piazza.

From the grandeur of St. Mark's Basilica to the intimacy of quiet backwater canals, Venice enchants with its timeless beauty and romantic atmosphere.

Best Time to Visit

April to June & September to October — Spring and autumn offer pleasant weather, manageable crowds, and lower prices. Summer (July-August) is hot and crowded but vibrant with festivals. Winter brings fog and acqua alta but magical festive charm.

Key Locations

  • St. Mark's Square — The heart of Venice
  • Grand Canal — Main waterway with palaces
  • Rialto Bridge — Iconic stone bridge & market
  • Burano — Colorful island of lace-makers
  • Murano — Famous glassblowing island
